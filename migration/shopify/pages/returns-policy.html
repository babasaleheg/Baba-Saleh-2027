Returns & Exchange Policy

Baba Saleh Sportswear

At Baba Saleh Sportswear, we are committed to providing you with high-quality sportswear and a great shopping experience. However, we understand that sometimes a product may not meet your expectations. That’s why we offer a clear and fair return policy to ensure your satisfaction and build your trust.

Eligibility for Returns

We accept returns under the following conditions:

  • Timeframe: Items must be returned within 7 days from the original date of purchase.

  • Receipt Requirement: You must present the original sales receipt (physical or digital) as proof of purchase. Returns without a receipt cannot be processed.

  • Product Condition: The item must be in its original, unused condition – unworn, unwashed, and free from any damage, stains, or odors. All original tags, labels, and packaging must be intact and included.

Non-Returnable Items

To maintain health and hygiene standards, the following items are not eligible for return unless faulty or defective:

  • Undergarments (e.g., sports bras, compression wear)

  • Socks

  • Any item that has been worn, washed, or altered

  • Items marked as "final sale" or "clearance"

Exchange Policy

If you wish to exchange an item for a different size or color, the same 7-day return window applies. Exchanges are subject to product availability at the time of return. If the desired item is unavailable, we may offer a store credit or process it as a return instead.

Refund Method

  • Approved returns will be refunded using the same payment method used in the original transaction.

  • If the original purchase was made via credit/debit card, please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to reflect in your account, depending on your bank's policies.

  • In the case of cash purchases, refunds will be issued in cash upon approval of the return.

How to Return

You can return items by visiting the same branch where the original purchase was made (e.g., the October branch). Our staff will inspect the item to ensure it meets the return criteria before proceeding.

Damaged or Faulty Items

If you receive an item that is damaged, defective, or not as described, please contact us within 48 hours of purchase. We will evaluate the issue and offer an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, repair, or full refund.
